Investing Growth Calculator

See how money grows with compound interest: when your interest starts earning interest too.

This is for learning only. Real investments can go up or down, and returns are never guaranteed. Always talk to a parent or a trusted adult before investing real money.

Investing Basics

  • Saving keeps your money safe. Investing puts your money to work so it can grow.
  • Compound interest: you earn interest on your money AND on the interest it already earned.
  • Start early: time is the biggest booster. Small amounts over many years beat big amounts over a few years.
  • Diversify: don't put all your money in one place. Spread it out to lower risk.
  • Risk and reward: higher possible returns usually come with higher risk of losing money.
  • Stay away from "get rich quick": if it promises huge, guaranteed returns fast, it's probably a scam.
